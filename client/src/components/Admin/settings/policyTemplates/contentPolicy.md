# Content Policy

This content policy is adapted from the Content Policy of Mirlo (https://mirlo.space), itself heavily influenced by the content policy of Ampwall.

Any content you submit to {{instanceName}} must comply with the following standards. This includes audio, images, text, video, and any future forms of content.

## Why do we have a content policy?

{{instanceName}} exists to help build thriving communities. We provide spaces for people to put music, images, and words on the internet. Listeners can also leave messages for artists, for example with a purchase or a tip, and write to them through contact forms. As much as we want everyone to be their true selves, some content must be excluded to keep things fun, welcoming, and legal for our core audience. This content policy exists to define the rules in as specific terms as possible.

This is a living document. It will grow and change over time.

## How we want to approach content moderation

{{instanceName}}'s content policy has a handful of goals:

- Provide a supportive environment for artists and music communities to thrive.
- Comply with our legal obligations.
- Comply with the content policy commitments made to our technology partners, such as payment processors.
- Protect the longevity of the platform so we can continue our mission.

We are aware that musicians hold a wide range of opinions about the appropriateness, offensiveness, and taste of many topics and beliefs. We will do our best to give space to as many communities as we can, and will default to the assumption of good faith and good intentions wherever possible.

{{instanceName}}'s preference is to apply rules based on content and behavior within and directly related to the platform. There are some exceptions where we may be forced to act based on behavior outside of {{instanceName}}. Some examples are:

- Actions and words of public figures outside of the platform, like a high-profile controversial figure who records an album of innocuous children's music.
- Promoting {{instanceName}} content alongside content that breaks other rules, like links to a {{instanceName}} page alongside hate speech and symbols.
- Severity or intensity of behavior outside the platform that severely disrupts our ability to provide service.

In some cases, aspects of our content policy might seem at odds with artistic expression. Art can deal with issues of violence, sexuality, and illegal acts without crossing the line of becoming excessive and unwelcome on {{instanceName}}. We trust our community members to use their best discretion when submitting content and reporting violations.

## House rules

In simple terms:

- No nazism, racism, xenophobic, sexist, transphobic, homophobic, or otherwise discriminatory and oppressive content, images, symbols, language, music, etc.
- No "dog whistle" content intending to mask or obscure any other prohibited topic.
- No content that will expose us, or you, to criminal liability in any way.
- No content that causes or incites specific harm or violence, including self-harm.
- No content or usage that bullies, harasses, or incites harassment.
- No advertising, selling, or promoting content that is unrelated to your artistic identity (spam, unrelated product marketing, etc.).
- No pornography, whether legal or illegal, in order to comply with payment processing terms.
- No images depicting excessive violence, abuse of animals, abuse of children, sexual abuse, or suicide.
- No sales of firearms, weapons of any kind, drugs, medicine, supplements, gambling, lotteries, or affiliate programs.
- No impersonation, misrepresentation of identity, or content that claims the identity of another without explicit approval.
- No auctions, raffles, affiliate programs, investment products, cryptocurrency, or NFTs.
- No spam, bullying, or "troll" behavior that deliberately hinders the ability of others to engage with the platform.

{{instanceName}} reserves the right to remove any content, at any time, for any reason, without notice or the possibility of appeal. We would prefer not to, so please do not make us.

## No illegal content

Do not upload anything to {{instanceName}} that violates a criminal law. Do not upload anything to {{instanceName}} that infringes on someone else's rights, including copyrights and trademarks.

A non-exhaustive list of illegal content that {{instanceName}} does not allow:

- Content that is defamatory
- Content that is libelous
- Content that aims to incite violent acts
- Stolen content
- Content you do not have the legal right to distribute
- Harassing or abusive content
- Child sexual abuse material

## No adult content

{{instanceName}} prohibits the posting, sharing, or dissemination of adult content. This is primarily because our payment processor does not allow its use for this purpose. For the purposes of this policy, "adult content" is defined as any material that is sexually explicit or pornographic, including but not limited to:

- Images, videos, or animations that depict graphic nudity, sexual acts, or sexually explicit material.
- Textual content that is intended to be sexually gratifying or that explicitly describes sexual acts.
- Content that promotes adult services, including but not limited to prostitution, escorts, erotic massages, and similar services.
- Materials that are considered obscene, lewd, lascivious, filthy, or pornographic.

## AI content

The emergence of AI technology such as Large Language Models (LLMs) poses complicated challenges for artists and platforms like {{instanceName}}. It is a rapidly moving field and policies will evolve as the technology and usage change. Creating one unified policy for everything AI does not reflect the nuance of the issue and risks being either too lenient or too restrictive.

{{instanceName}}'s priority is preventing human artists from being forced to appear alongside or compete with low-effort AI-generated works. For that reason we do not allow the addition of music that is entirely created by AI. We recognize that AI can be used creatively but are very conscious of many large language models' catastrophic energy costs. However, it is incredibly hard for us to moderate or know what music is generated by an LLM, and we ask musicians to reflect seriously on how the music or art they are making expresses their creativity.

Where images are concerned (artwork on albums, merch, etc., as well as logos and artist profile images), similar rules apply.

## Physical merchandise guidelines

Where permitted to upload, list, and sell physical goods, additional policies and guidelines apply.

{{instanceName}}'s e-commerce pages are for original music and music community-affiliated transactions. List and sell items that pertain to your musical identity. This can be broadly defined, but we reserve the right to remove items that are outside the mission of the platform.

- No weapons, fireworks, explosives, or anything that can cause injury.
- No live or dead animals.
- No perishable food items.
- No unauthorized (bootleg) merchandise from other artists.
- No "vintage stores" or similar resellers of recycled goods.
- Instruments and equipment are permitted when the seller is the manufacturer or an official distributor.

## Expectations in messages

When communicating with others on {{instanceName}}, all participants are required to be considerate of the people around them. In other words, don't be a jerk, don't be a troll, don't be deliberately disruptive. This is a vague area that will be defined more explicitly as time goes on. In general, the expectations are:

- Assume positive intent when reading the messages of others.
- Avoid "flame wars" and engaging with bad faith comments.
- Use the available reporting features as necessary.
- Avoid posting deliberately inflammatory content that aims to be disruptive to {{instanceName}} and its users.
- Don't use {{instanceName}} for political or ideological battles. Healthy discussion is welcome in appropriate spaces. Read the room.
- Respect requested pronouns and gender identities.
- Above all else, do not ever be needlessly rude, cruel, or disrespectful of another {{instanceName}} user's work in a message to them.

## How we respond to reports

When considering whether reported content is truly a violation, we will consider a multitude of factors. Not all of this information will be publicly available, and we ask that you interpret our decisions with the same level of good faith that we will interpret your artwork and uploaded content.

When {{instanceName}}, or anyone empowered to act on behalf of {{instanceName}} as part of our moderation efforts or legal obligations, feels that content or a user violates the above rules, we may take any number of actions in response. These may take the shape of:

- Limiting the visibility of the content
- Asking them to remove the content
- Forcefully removing the content
- Temporarily placing restrictions on the account
- Removing the account entirely from the platform

We will strive to treat all users with respect in these cases.

## How to report a violation

Links to report violations of our rules are available in some places on {{instanceName}}. For other cases, please email us at {{contactEmail}}. Use your {{instanceName}} account whenever possible and please provide relevant information. We cannot guarantee responses to every message or an explanation of actions taken, but we will do our best.

{{instanceName}} reserves the right to remove any content that violates this content policy without notice. Further, users who repeatedly violate this policy may face suspension or termination of their account. We rely on our community to help maintain the standards we set and encourage users to report any content that breaks these rules through our reporting tools. {{instanceName}} is committed to reviewing reported content and taking appropriate action in a timely manner. Please note that this policy is part of our broader effort to create a safe and legal platform for users of all ages. We appreciate your cooperation in adhering to these guidelines.
