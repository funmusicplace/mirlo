# Listening in Subsonic apps

You can play the releases and tracks you've bought on Mirlo in any app that supports the Subsonic API. Feishin, Amperfy, Symfonium, play:Sub and Ultrasonic are some examples. Mirlo acts as the server, so the app streams your music straight from Mirlo.

## Connecting an app

1. Open your [account settings](https://mirlo.space/account) and find "Subsonic apps".
2. Choose "Generate credentials".
3. In the app, add a server. Enter the server address from the page, then your username and password. On mirlo.space the server address is `https://mirlo.space/subsonic`.
4. Your purchases appear in the app as albums and artists.

Each value on the page has a copy button, so you don't have to type the password by hand.

## What you'll find

- Only music you've bought shows up.
- Single tracks show up as their album, with just the tracks you own.
- Streams play the original files the artist uploaded.
- Playlists, favorites and lyrics aren't available yet.

## Revoking and regenerating

Choose "Revoke" to remove access for every app at once.

Choose "Generate new password" to remove access for every app and get a new password. Enter the new password in each app you still want to use.

Apps store your password so they can reconnect without asking. If you lose a device that has a Subsonic app on it, revoke your credentials right away.
