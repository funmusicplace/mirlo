# For labels

Label profiles are free to all users--a label profile gets all the benefits of an [artist profile](./for-artists) plus some more! See the [How To on label accounts](../how-tos/label-accounts.md) to get started.

## Your roster

### Showcase your artists

Showcase the entire roster of artists on your profile page, in the
order you choose. Each artist keeps their own page; your label page
brings them together.

### Bring artists on board

There's more than one way to build a roster.

Invite an artist who's already on Mirlo and they'll get an email
asking them to join — it takes both of you agreeing, so nobody ends
up on a roster they didn't opt into. If the artist isn't on Mirlo
yet, you can create their page yourself and get their music up now;
they can claim it with their own account later. Artists you already
manage under your own account can be added to the roster directly.

### Let artists find you

Make your label searchable and artists can come to you, sending a
request to join your roster instead of waiting for an invitation.

## Managing artists

### Decide who can do what

You can manage artist accounts you add as well as artists from other
users who give you permission to manage their artists.

Permissions aren't all-or-nothing. An artist can let you add
releases to their page without handing over the rest of their
account, or give you full management of it. Artists also choose
whether your label is shown on their page.

### Work across your whole roster

Your management area gathers your roster's work in one place:
published releases and the ones still in progress, so you can see
what's ready to go out and what's still waiting on someone.

## Payments

### Choose who gets paid

The label feature people ask for most: for each release and each
merch item, you decide whether the money goes to the label's payment
account or straight to the artist's.

That means you can carry the payment setup for artists who don't
have their own, and hand it back whenever the artist is ready. If an
artist on your roster hasn't set up payments yet, Mirlo points them
to you.

### Sales and orders across the label

Sales for every artist you manage land in one place, filterable down
to a single artist when you need it, with the platform and payment
processor cuts broken out and a spreadsheet export for your
accounting.

Merch orders work the same way — one queue for the whole roster, so
shipping day is one job rather than one job per artist.

## Your label page

### Brand it

Your label page is an artist page, so everything on the
[artist features page](./for-artists) applies: custom colors and
backgrounds, renamed and reordered tabs, announcements, links, and
posts that go out to your subscribers.

### Embed your catalog

Put your roster on your own website with a label embed, alongside
the individual track and release embeds.
